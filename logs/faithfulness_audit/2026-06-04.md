## Root cause 1 — CRF contrast axis is [0.01,1], author uses [1e-5,1] / [1e-4,0.1]  (CODE_BUG, figure)

`protocols._contrast_sweep` (line 28) hardcodes `logspace(log10(0.01), log10(1.0), n)` and the view
(`article_aware/views.py` lines 66-74) pins x to (0.01, 1.0) for every CRF panel. The author scripts
use `cRange = [1e-5 1]` (Figure2A.m, Figure3C.m) and `[1e-4 0.1]` (Figure4C.m, Figure4E.m). The model
half-saturates at c≈0.0027 (verified), so the entire rising limb — and the contrast-gain left-shift
that 2A/2B/3C/3F are *about* — lives BELOW 0.01 and is clipped off-screen. The rendered CRFs look like
near-flat plateaus; the paper shows full sigmoids. This is the single cause of the 2A/2B/3C/3F
left-shift reds. **Fix:** per-panel contrast range from the author script (2A/2B/3C/3F → [1e-5,1];
4C/4E → [1e-4,0.1]) for BOTH the sweep and the view xlim; re-digitize over that range.

## Root cause 2 — digitized reference traced over [0.01,1] not the author axis  (figure)

`figure_2/panel_A_digitized.json x_range=[0.01,1.0]` (and siblings). The paper has NO numeric x
labels; the digitizer assumed the left edge ≈ 0.01, but the author axis floor is 1e-5. The digitized
"rise at c≈0.05-0.10" is therefore mis-placed ~20-30× too high — this is what the README's SQ-007
Gap-1 misread as "the model's left-shift sits below the window." **Fix:** re-digitize 2A/2B/3C/3F/4C/4E
with x_range = author cRange; the model's half-max c≈0.003 then lands inside the window.

## Root cause 3 — protocols co-locate / over-simplify stimulus geometry  (CODE_BUG, figure: 4C, 4E, 7C)

The author's two-stimulus-in-RF panels place the stimuli at SEPARATED x positions; the impl puts both
at x=0. With σ_stim=5 and a unit grid, co-located stimuli overlap heavily, so feature competition
crushes the nonpreferred response and the modulation overflows.
- **4E** (`protocols.run_figure_4E`): impl = 2 stim both at x=0 (θ 0/180). Author Figure4E.m = 4 stim at
  x=90/110 (RF, θ 0/180) and x=-90/-110 (contralateral), RF_center=100, Apeak=5, AxWidth=5,
  AthetaWidth=20, cRange[1e-4,0.1]. Author-geometry rerun → %-mod 52% (impl 386%). **Fix:** use the
  four-stimulus separated layout + [1e-4,0.1].
- **7C** (`protocols.run_figure_7C`): impl = 2 stim both at x=0, fixation at x=50. Author Figure7C.m =
  variable stim at x=93, null (θ=180) at x=107, RF_center=100, attend-away at x=-100, Apeak=5,
  AxWidth=5, AthetaWidth=45, oval shape. Author-geometry rerun → var/fix 1.41 (impl 2.73). **Fix:**
  use the x=93/107 separated layout and attend-away at -100.
- **4C** (`protocols.run_figure_4C`): impl = attend-RF facilitation, single recorded condition,
  %-mod=(att-ign)/ign positive. Author Figure4C.m = attend the NONPREFERRED stim in RF (null fixed at
  contrast 0.01), 4 separated stimuli, and %-mod=(unatt-att)/unatt (attending the null SUPPRESSES the
  preferred neuron). Wrong condition mapping + wrong geometry + wrong sign. **Fix:** adopt the author's
  attend-nonpreferred protocol and suppression %-mod sign.
