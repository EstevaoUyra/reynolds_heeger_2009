VALUE shifts ~3 decades — which is exactly what moves the model's c≈0.003 half-max
from "off-window/clipped" to "in-window/visible." In the SAME commit, by the SAME

Why it bends toward green: the reference the loosened thresholds are validated

This is NOT a clear leniency call — the no-numeric-x-ticks admission makes the prior

threshold-loosen-plus-reference-re-trace in one self-graded commit, taken in lieu of

Recommendation: route to the **Faithfulness Auditor** — (a) confirm the paper's Fig
