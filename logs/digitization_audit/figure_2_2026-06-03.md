## PANEL 2A — STATUS: FAITHFUL-DIGITIZATION (with one minor honesty caveat)

- **Normalization / frame — FAITHFUL.** Solid CRFs sit at 0.615 on an axis anchored to
  the labelled 0 and 1; NOT pinned to 1.0. Shared sub-1.0 scale confirmed. 1.0-pinning
  is genuinely fixed here.
- **Plateau — FAITHFUL.** Independent trace 0.615; candidate 0.615. Both solids coincide
  at plateau (paper shows a thin double line at row ~152–153; matches).
- **% modulation dashed — FAITHFUL.** My overlay of the candidate's dashed lands on the
  paper's dashed across the whole range (high ~98% at low contrast → ~0% by mid/high
  contrast). Good.
- **attended/ignored split — MINOR / honesty caveat (UNVERIFIED-as-stated, not a hard
  divergence).** In the paper the two solid CRFs are nearly coincident with a small
  leftward (contrast-gain) shift of attended; the tracer cannot cleanly separate two
  same-colour near-coincident curves (the skill's documented LIMITATION). The candidate's
  mid-rise split is in the CORRECT direction (attended left/above ignored) and of modest
  magnitude (Δy ~0.05–0.10 around x≈0.05–0.16), and the JSON description honestly scopes
  it ("only metrically supported in mid-rise, envelope elsewhere"). However, in my
  re-overlay the green (ignored) curve sits visibly below/right of the paper's solid
  bundle through much of the rise, suggesting the split magnitude is somewhat OVERSTATED
  even if the sign is right. Severity: minor. Because it is explicitly caveated in the
  JSON as interpolated/envelope outside the mid-rise, this is acceptable-with-caveat
  rather than invented — but the rendered offset is a touch larger than pixels support.
  No TOOL-MISUSE: tools were used and the split is honestly scoped.

## PANEL 2B — STATUS: DIGITIZATION-DIVERGENT (major) — solids faithful, % modulation wrong

- **Solid CRFs — FAITHFUL.** Attended plateau 0.859 (candidate 0.852–0.858), ignored
  plateau 0.601 (candidate 0.600–0.605). Sustained ~0.25 separation with no convergence
  at high contrast — the response-gain signature — confirmed by independent traces
  (attended row ~82 vs ignored row ~157 hold apart out to the right frame). The two solids
  overlay tightly on the paper. Good.
- **Normalization / frame — FAITHFUL.** Same anchored 0–1 axis; plateaus at 0.86/0.60,
  not 1.0. Shared sub-1.0 scale with 2A confirmed; 2B attended > 2A attended on one axis.
- **% modulation dashed — DIVERGENT (major).** Candidate JSON has the dashed recovering to
  ~79–81% and staying elevated at high contrast. The PAPER's dashed at high contrast sits
  at **~42–45%** (pixel row ~210 → 0.42; isolated dashed-band trace cols 0.15–1.0 gives
  44%, 42%, 42%, 46%). The candidate's right-side % curve is roughly DOUBLED / inverted
  vs the paper — see my re-overlay `/tmp/myoverlay_B.png`: the blue candidate curve floats
  far above the paper's dashed across the entire right half. The dashed in the paper
  descends from ~99% at low contrast, through the crossing, and continues DOWN to a low
  ~40% plateau; it does NOT "recover and stay elevated ~80%." The committed `overlay_2B.png`
  shows the same gap but the digitizer kept the JSON values anyway.
  - Severity: **major**. The two solid CRFs (the panel's primary scientific content) are
    fine, but the attentional-modulation curve — a labelled right-axis quantity — is
    mis-digitized by ~35–40 percentage points over the entire high-contrast range, and the
    crossing-region shape (dip-then-recover) is asserted where the pixels show a monotone
    decline. The digitizer flagged the dashed-crossing region as "least certain"; the error
    is larger and more systematic than that caveat implies.
