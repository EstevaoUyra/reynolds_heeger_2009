## Panel 4C — STATUS: DIGITIZATION-DIVERGENT (major) + provenance gap

### Faithful dimensions
- **Normalization is faithful (sub-1.0).** Solids plateau at ~0.76/0.75 (digitized)
  vs my trace ~0.78/0.74. NOT auto-normalized to 1.0. Good — the shared-scale trap
  is avoided.
- **Right-axis modulation faithful.** Digitized dashed 36.4% → 4.8%. My trace of the
  declining dashed: ~37% at low contrast → ~5% at the right. Monotone decline,
  within [0,100]. Matches.
- Both solids sigmoidal, lower solid traced well by the digitized "unattended".

### Divergence — the two-solid GAP is collapsed to ~1/3–1/4 of the paper's
The digitizer's claim/description ("two solids nearly OVERLIE, split ±~0.022 only
mid-upper") **understates the real separation.** A 6× zoom of the rising region
(cols 118–178) shows two clearly distinct parallel curves with a visible white gap
through the whole rising portion and into the upper-right. My trace vs digitized split
(left-axis units), at matched x:

| x (contrast) | digitized split | my-trace split |
|---|---|---|
| 0.149 | +0.023 | **+0.095** |
| 0.229 | +0.029 | **+0.101** |
| 0.352 | +0.026 | **+0.091** |
| 0.437 | +0.020 | **+0.082** |
| 0.542 | +0.016 | **+0.068** |
| 0.673 | +0.012 | **+0.055** |
| 0.835 | +0.010 | **+0.042** |

The paper gap is ~0.07–0.10 in the mid-rise and still ~0.04 near the plateau; the
digitized gap is ~0.01–0.03 — roughly a **3–5× under-statement.** Additionally the
digitized **upper** ("attended") curve sits ~0.04–0.05 LOW vs the paper's upper solid
(e.g. plateau digit 0.748 vs trace 0.782; at x=0.229 digit 0.448 vs trace 0.500);
the lower curve matches well. Net effect: the digitizer fit BOTH curves close to the
paper's *lower* solid and shrank the gap.

**Severity: major.** Fig 4C's content claim is the attentional CRF offset (contrast-
gain: attention shifts/raises the CRF). Collapsing the gap to ~0.02 understates that
attentional effect by 3–4×. A tier test graded against this ruler would pass an
implementation whose 4C attentional modulation is far too small, or fail a faithful
one — exactly the mis-calibrated-instrument failure mode the skill guards.

### Minor
- Digitized C modulation has a small non-monotone wobble at x≈0.11–0.15 (21.2→23.4).
  The paper dashed declines monotonically there; the bump is a tracing artifact where
  the rising solids cross the dashed. Small (~2–3%), minor.
