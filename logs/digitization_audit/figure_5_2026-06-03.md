## Panel 5C — STATUS: DIGITIZATION-DIVERGENT (major) + TOOL-MISUSE (the fold)

The candidate's **scalar facts at the apex are faithful**, but two declared
**method shortcuts assert structure the pixels contradict or do not support**:
the curve is provably **right-skewed**, so the **symmetric fold about x=0 is a
fabricated symmetry** (DIGITIZATION-DIVERGENT, up to ~0.13 normalized units on
the flanks), and the **unattended = attended×0.857 construction** is an
assume-the-conclusion shortcut (its result happens to match the pixels, but it
was asserted, not measured — TOOL-MISUSE / honesty concern).

### What I verified against the paper pixels (independent re-measurement)

Calibration anchors I established from the panel:
- y: top frame row 6 = 1.0, bottom frame row 147 = 0.0 (long dark horizontal runs).
- x: the **five orientation glyphs** below the plot center at cols
  67.4 / 111.5 / **156.0** / 200.4 / 242.3; central (vertical-bars) glyph col 156 = 0°.
  Glyph spacing ≈ 44 px. **Frame-center (col 136) is NOT 0°** — see calibration note.

**1. Peak heights & ratio — FAITHFUL.** Independently traced apexes (text-masked):
attended apex y = **0.968** (col 153), unattended apex y = **0.833** (col 152),
ratio **1.162**. Matches the candidate (0.968 / 0.83 / 1.16) almost exactly.
Attended peak sits just below the 1.0 frame and does not touch it — correct.

**2. Normalization — FAITHFUL.** Peaks read against the shared B/C
"Normalized Response" frame (0..1). No per-panel auto-normalize-to-1.0; attended
correctly stops at ~0.97. Good.

**3. Width / "same FWHM, no sharpening" — FAITHFUL (independently confirmed).**
I measured FWHM of each curve from the pixels *without* using the candidate's
assumed ratio: attended FWHM = **82.0 px**, unattended FWHM = **81.9 px** →
ratio **1.001**. In the region where the two curves are separately resolved, the
measured unatt/att ratio is constant at **0.853–0.867** across the full span
(cols 120–192). So the paper genuinely shows a near-constant vertical scaling
with equal width — the "pure multiplicative, same bandwidth" reading is correct.
(My first visual impression that the attended curve was "broader" was contamination
from the y-axis label text; the clean trace refutes it.)

**4. CURVE IS RIGHT-SKEWED — the symmetry is not in the paper (DIGITIZATION-DIVERGENT).**
The candidate JSON is *perfectly* symmetric (att(x) − att(−x) = 0.0000 at every
point). The paper curve is not. Measured symmetrically about the true apex
(col 153), the **right flank is consistently higher than the left flank** at
matched distance:

| Δ from apex (px) | left-flank y | right-flank y | (L − R) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | 0.837 | 0.904 | −0.067 |
| 24 | 0.720 | 0.816 | −0.096 |
| 32 | 0.578 | 0.695 | −0.117 |
| 40 | 0.443 | 0.553 | −0.110 |
| 48 | 0.306 | 0.433 | −0.127 |
| 56 | 0.214 | 0.312 | −0.098 |

The asymmetry is robust across contour levels (right/left half-width ratio = 1.27 / 1.18 / 1.13
at 75% / 50% / 25% of max) and independent of x-calibration (measured about the
apex itself). The paper bell has a steeper left rise and a longer right tail.

**5. THE FOLD (occlude-fill about x=0) — TOOL-MISUSE / fabricated structure.**
The far left tail (cols ≈ 58–72, x ≲ −80°) is genuinely occluded by the "C"
label and the rotated "Normalized Response" axis text — the upper-band trace
there returns spurious y ≈ 0.91 (text, not curve), so the digitizer was right
that it could not be cleanly traced. **But** the *recoverable* left flank (cols
95–151, below the C) is NOT occluded, and folding the right tail onto it imposes
a symmetry the pixels deny (finding #4). A faithful fill would have traced the
recoverable left flank directly and only inter/extrapolated the small occluded
far-tail; mirroring the whole left side **fabricates a symmetric curve**. Absolute
flank error from the imposed symmetry is up to ~0.10–0.13 normalized units in the
mid-flank. Severity: major (it falsifies a measurable shape feature, even though
the apex and width are right).

**6. The "unattended = attended × 0.857" construction — TOOL-MISUSE (assume-the-conclusion).**
The unattended curve was not traced; it was generated as attended × 0.857
(ratio = 0.857 to 4 dp at every point). The model's *claim* under test is exactly
"attention is pure response-gain (constant multiplicative ratio, same width)."
Building the reference curve by *assuming* that ratio makes the digitized
reference incapable of ever falsifying the model on that axis — the ruler bakes in
the conclusion it is supposed to let the tier-tests check. **Mitigation:** I
measured the ratio independently from the resolved pixels and it *is* ~0.855–0.867
and constant, and FWHM ratio is 1.001 — so the construction's *output* is faithful
to the paper here. The finding is methodological/honesty, not a value divergence:
the curve should be traced, then the constant ratio *reported as a measured
finding*, not asserted into existence. As-is it is an unauditable shortcut.

**7. x-calibration latent risk (note, not a standalone divergence).** The
candidate data is symmetric about x = 0, and x = 0 = central glyph = col 156,
but the true apex is col 153 (slightly left of 0°). My re-overlay using the
**frame-center** x-calibration (col 136 = 0°) is clearly mis-registered (curves
shifted left of the paper); the **glyph-anchored** calibration (col 156 = 0°)
matches well. The candidate's own overlay matched, so it used the correct
(glyph/peak) anchoring — good — but the peak-at-exactly-0 assumption is part of
the same imposed symmetry as findings #4–#5.

## Process / provenance

- **No provenance/tool-trail file** accompanies the retrial (`_retrial/` holds only
  the JSON and overlay PNG). The figure-type → tools → why rationale is unrecorded;
  the only "trail" is the prose in the JSON `description`. Unauditable tool choice —
  a process finding on its own (skill Step 2 / Output).
- The 23 evenly-spaced, perfectly-symmetric points with an exact ×0.857 second
  curve are the classic **eyeball/construct tell**: the unattended curve and the
  left half of the attended curve were generated, not traced. The tracer
  (`trace_darkest_in_band`) was available and works on the resolved regions
  (I used it); it was not used to independently produce the unattended curve or the
  recoverable left flank.

## Refute pass

- "Attended is broader" → **withdrawn**; FWHM ratio measures 1.001, equal width is faithful.
- "Peaks/ratio wrong" → **withdrawn**; independently confirmed (0.968 / 0.833 / 1.16).
- "Right-skew" → **survives**; consistent across three contour levels and measured
  about the apex (calibration-independent), 0.07–0.13 normalized-unit flank gap.
- "Fold fabricates symmetry" → **survives**; recoverable (non-occluded) left flank
  contradicts the mirrored right tail.
- "×0.857 assumes the conclusion" → **survives** as method/honesty finding; downgraded
  in *severity* because the measured ratio happens to confirm the value.

## Verdict

**Do NOT green panel 5C.** Status **DIGITIZATION-DIVERGENT (major) + TOOL-MISUSE.**
The apex heights, peak ratio, normalization, and equal-width reading are faithful
and independently confirmed. But the reference **imposes two structures the pixels
do not support**: (a) a symmetric fold onto a curve that is measurably right-skewed
(fabricated symmetry, recoverable left flank contradicts it), and (b) an unattended
curve constructed as attended×0.857 rather than traced (assume-the-conclusion; its
value is faithful but the method is unauditable and cannot falsify the model's
gain claim). Both are honesty/process concerns even though the result "looks
plausible." Recommend: re-trace the unattended curve and the recoverable left flank
with `trace_darkest_in_band`; allow asymmetry; report the ratio/width as *measured*;
inter/extrapolate only the small "C"-occluded far-left tail and mark it as such;
record the figure-type→tools→why provenance.
