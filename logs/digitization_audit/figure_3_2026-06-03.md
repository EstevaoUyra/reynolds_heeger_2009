## Panel 3C — STATUS: DIGITIZATION-DIVERGENT (minor–moderate)

What is faithful:
- Plateau heights (0.93–0.94, sub-1.0) — match (see above).
- Foot: at x≈0.01 both solids at y≈0.235; paper foot in the x≈0.017–0.025 band
  reads y≈0.24–0.26. Match.
- Two solids coincident at low contrast (both start at 0.235), small attended-above
  separation through the mid-rise, re-converging near the plateau. The paper
  (mid-rise crop) shows exactly this: two closely-spaced lines, attended leading.
  **The "envelope split" is honestly scoped — both curves share the same foot value
  and diverge by only ~0.03–0.05; it is not an invented offset.**
- Completeness/mapping: 3 curves (attended/unattended left axis, percent_modulation
  right axis), log-x, correct ranges. Correct.

Divergences found (survive refute pass):

1. **Rising flank sits too high / sigmoid c50 too low (moderate).**
   I compared the digitized *attended* against the paper attended **top-edge**
   (the centerline is even lower, so the gap below is a lower bound):
   - x≈0.057: paper top-edge ≈0.41, JSON 0.537 → JSON ~0.13 high.
   - x≈0.073: paper top-edge ≈0.52, JSON 0.635 → ~0.12 high.
   - x≈0.093: paper top-edge ≈0.64, JSON 0.736 → ~0.10 high.
   Endpoints (foot, plateau) match, so this is not a scale/calibration error — the
   whole sigmoid is shifted toward lower contrast by ~0.02 in c50. My overlay shows
   the red/green curves riding above the black solids through the rise. Severity:
   moderate (steep region, so a small x-shift inflates the y-gap).

2. **Dashed % bump has a kink/flat-top artifact and apex shifted slightly left (minor).**
   The JSON has duplicated 27.0 points at x=0.0354 and 0.0421, producing a visible
   flat-top/notch on the bump's left flank (clear in my overlay). My pixel trace of
   the dashed apex puts the smooth peak at x≈0.045–0.055, top-edge ≈30–31%
   (centerline ≈28–29%). JSON peak ≈27% at x≈0.035–0.042 — peak *height* is fine,
   but the apex is ~one log-step left of the paper and rendered with a kink rather
   than a smooth bell. Severity: minor.
