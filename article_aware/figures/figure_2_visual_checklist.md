## Coordinate convention

Both panels share the same axis layout:

- **Horizontal axis (log contrast):** stimulus contrast on a logarithmic scale. The axis
  has no numeric labels — only relative positions (low contrast = left, high contrast =
  right) matter for checklist items.
- **Left vertical axis:** normalized model response, ranging from 0 (bottom) to 1 (top).
- **Right vertical axis:** attentional modulation in percent, ranging from 0 to 100%.
- **Curve encoding:** attended = solid dark/thick curve; ignored (unattended) = solid
  thin/lighter curve; percent modulation = dashed curve.
- **"Left-shifted"** means the attended curve reaches its half-maximum at a lower contrast
  (further left on the x-axis) than the ignored curve.
- **"Upward-shifted"** means the attended curve is higher than the ignored curve at the
  same contrast, without a substantial lateral displacement.

### CRF curves
- [ ] Two sigmoidal (S-shaped, monotonically increasing and saturating) curves are present.
- [ ] The attended curve is shifted leftward relative to the ignored curve on the log-
